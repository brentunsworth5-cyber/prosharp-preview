About ProSharp

Sharpening with respect for the tool.

ProSharp serves working hair, barber and grooming professionals with careful edge restoration, tool maintenance and practical service.

A sharper tool is only useful if it cuts the way it was designed to.

That is why ProSharp looks beyond basic sharpness. Blade geometry, tension, balance, finish and intended use all matter.

Our felt and water honing approach uses controlled hand honing without harsh abrasives. Service may also include cleaning, adjustment and careful evaluation of damage or wear.

What guides the work

  • Evaluate first
    Identify the edge, condition and performance issue before removing metal.
  • Match the method
    Use a sharpening approach appropriate to the tool and intended work.
  • Finish the whole tool
    Check movement, tension, balance and cutting performance—not only the edge.

Have a tool you are unsure about?

Describe the issue and ProSharp can help you choose the right next step.
